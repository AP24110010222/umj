About Uma Maheshwari Jewellers

Conveniently situated at the heart of the town at Gandhi Chowk, Sattenapalli, Uma Maheshwari Jewellers stands as a trusted destination for families celebrating life's most precious milestones.

We take pride in offering quality jewellery that seamlessly blends authentic heritage artistry with modern sensibilities. From delicate daily-wear chains and rings to opulent bridal harams and temple bangles, each piece is handcrafted to uphold supreme standards of purity, design distinction, and lasting value.

Our commitment is built on transparent relationships, honest weight calculations, and genuine customer satisfaction for every patron who walks through our doors.
